This page carries two kinds of note. The first is mechanical: every domain page and every page provider's index page has a reviewed title and lede snapshot in review_state.yaml, and a page whose current title or lede no longer matches its snapshot is flagged below automatically at generation time, with the same banner shown on the page itself. The second is editorial: action items in inbox.yaml, added as suggested and moved to accepted or dismissed only by the owner.
