The palette is closed: eight inks, named for their job, such as {{ink.accent}} for
headings and danger. Adding a ninth is a decision, not an edit.

A ramp is a short hand-picked run of stops, not a formula — the accent ramp exists
because a hover or a chip needs a step between two inks, not because a gradient
generator produced one.
